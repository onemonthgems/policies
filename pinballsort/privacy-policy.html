Flipper Sort: Neon Pinball — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · App package: com.onemonthgems.pinballsort

OMGems (“the developer”) respects the privacy of everyone who plays the mobile game “Flipper Sort: Neon Pinball” (“the app”). This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and with whom it is shared.

1. Personal information the developer collects

The developer does not collect or store personal information such as your name, email address or phone number. The app has no sign-up or login, and it has no in-app purchases or other payments.

Your game data (stage progress, endless-mode best score, difficulty choices, the time left on a banner break, and settings such as language, music, sound and vibration) is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer. The developer runs no servers for the app. The data is deleted when you uninstall the app. If Android backup is turned on, this data may be included in your Google account backup.

2. Information collected to show ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) by Google LLC to show three kinds of ads:

  • A banner ad at the bottom of the screen.
  • Full-screen (interstitial) ads after you clear a stage, before an endless-mode game starts, and when you restart a game from the in-game settings.
  • An optional rewarded ad. If you choose to watch it to the end, the bottom banner is hidden for a limited time. Full-screen ads still appear while the banner is hidden.

The SDK automatically collects the following information and shares it with Google.

ItemDetails
IP addressMay be used to estimate the general location of the device.
App interactionsApp launches, taps, ad views and similar interactions
DiagnosticsPerformance information such as launch time, app-not-responding rate and energy use
Device and account identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers related to the account signed in on the device

This information is used to show ads, measure ad performance, analytics and fraud prevention. It is encrypted in transit (TLS). For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for consent. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google’s consent form is shown before ads are requested. Where that form applies, you can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Ad privacy settings.

3. Managing your advertising ID

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings under Privacy > Ads (menu names may differ by device). A deleted advertising ID is no longer used for personalized ads.

4. App permissions

  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used to load ads.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): used for a short vibration when a stage starts and when a color sensor on the table is triggered. Vibration is off until you turn it on in Settings.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): used by the ads SDK to show and measure ads.
  • Keeping work running and foreground service (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): added by the ads SDK for its background tasks. The game itself does not use them.

The app does not request sensitive permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos and files.

5. Sharing with third parties

Apart from the data sent to Google through the ads SDK described above, the developer does not share or sell your information to third parties.

6. Retention and deletion

The developer keeps no personal information. Game data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app. Information collected by Google is retained and deleted under Google’s policies.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed primarily at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you learn that information about a child under 13 has been collected, please contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
