Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026
App: Forest Expedition (숲의 원정)
Package: com.omgems.multiidle
Developer: OMGems

This policy explains how OMGems handles information in Forest Expedition (the “App”).

1. Information handled by the App

The App does not require an account or sign-in. OMGems does not collect personal information through the App, and the App does not send gameplay data to an OMGems server.

Hero settings, equipped items, skill choices, stage selections, difficulty, and boss challenge settings are stored locally on your device so that they can be restored when you return. These records are not an online account and are not synchronized by OMGems.

2. Advertising, analytics, and purchases

This version does not include advertising, in-app purchases, third-party analytics, or cloud crash reporting. It does not access your advertising identifier.

3. Device access

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library. Local game records use the App’s own storage.

4. Sharing and retention

OMGems does not sell or share personal data collected through the App because the App does not collect such data. Local game records remain on your device until you clear the App’s storage or remove the App. Platform-level services are governed by the policies and settings of the platform provider.

5. Your choices and deletion

You can erase local game records using Android Settings, then Apps, then Forest Expedition, then Storage, then Clear storage. Menu names may vary. This permanently resets your local progress. OMGems cannot restore local records that have been erased.

6. Children’s privacy

The App does not ask players, including children, to submit personal information. If you believe personal information has been provided to OMGems in connection with the App, please contact us so that we can address the request.

7. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use that information only to respond to your inquiry and retain it only as needed to resolve the inquiry or meet legal obligations. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us.
