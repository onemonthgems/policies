Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · App: Rescue Animal: FREE THE WILD (com.rescueanimal.game)

OMGems (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the privacy of everyone who plays the mobile game Rescue Animal: FREE THE WILD (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and with whom it is shared.

1. Personal Information We Collect Directly

We do not directly collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The App has no account registration or sign-in.

Your game progress (rescued animals, Tree of Life growth, settings, and so on) is stored on your device and is not sent to our servers. It is deleted from your device when you delete the App. However, if Android backup is turned on, this data may be included in your Google account backup.

2. Information Collected for Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) provided by Google LLC to display ads. The SDK automatically collects the following information and shares it with Google.

DataDescription
IP addressMay be used to estimate the general location of the device.
App interactionsApp launches, taps, ad views, and other interaction information
DiagnosticsPerformance information such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage
Device and account identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID, and, where applicable, identifiers related to the account signed in on the device

This information is used for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. It is encrypted in transit (TLS). For more details, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Managing Your Advertising ID

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device’s Settings > Privacy > Ads (menu names may differ by device). Once deleted, your advertising ID is no longer used for personalized ads.

4. App Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used to load ads.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad-related features (ad attribution, topics): used by the ads SDK to serve and measure ads.
  • Other system permissions, such as keeping work running while the device sleeps: used for the ads SDK’s background tasks.

The App does not request sensitive permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos and files.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

Other than the transfer to Google through the ads SDK described above, we do not provide or sell your information to any third party.

6. Retention and Deletion

We do not retain any personal information. Game progress on your device is deleted when you delete the App. Information collected by Google is retained and deleted in accordance with Google’s policies.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you learn that information from a child under 13 has been collected, please contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date.
